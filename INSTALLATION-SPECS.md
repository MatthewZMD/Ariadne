# Ariadne — proposed gallery installation

This is a provisional physical presentation of the existing browser artwork, not documentation of a fabricated installation. The work is encountered at a freestanding exhibition table: a CRT monitor runs the actual game, with wired headphones, keyboard, and mouse. There is no desk or chair. The title-screen texture is the existing `public/ariadne-title-card.png`; no gameplay screenshot is fabricated.

## Space, components, and materials

Coordinate origin: centre of the table footprint, finished floor Z=0, visitor on negative Y. Minimum clear room: 4.5 × 4.5 × 3.2 m. Keep a 1.5 m preferred viewing and operating zone on the visitor side and a 900 mm clear route beside it. The exhibition table is 1400 × 750 × 35 mm, centred at (0,0,0.745) m, with a birch-veneer top, 3 mm eased edges, four 38 mm square powder-coated steel legs at (±0.62,±0.30) m, and rubber feet in floor contact. The tabletop is 780 mm high. Its rear cable trough is 1100 × 55 × 45 mm at (0,0.286,0.695) m; a 65 mm grommet at (0.48,0.18,0.766) m receives cables.

The 4:3 CRT monitor is centred at (0,0.08,1.105) m. The ABS enclosure is 530 × 470 × 430 mm, supported by a 310 × 270 mm foot and 100 × 120 × 180 mm neck. Its black front bezel is 465 × 345 mm. Ariadne's existing 16:9 title card fills a 382 × 215 mm recessed picture area within the 4:3 face and sits behind curved anti-glare glass. Use a tested, electrically safe CRT or a CRT-profiled display enclosure only after confirming venue electrical requirements, brightness, ventilation, and service access. Target display brightness is 80–120 cd/m²; test legibility and reflections with the actual game before production.

The wired keyboard is 360 × 125 × 20 mm centred at (−0.12,−0.205,0.775) m. The wired mouse is centred at (0.31,−0.205,0.783) m. Wired over-ear headphones rest on the tabletop at x=0.36–0.47 m, y=0.105 m; their cable lies on the table and plugs into the CRT front jack at (0.18,−0.183,0.98) m. Keyboard and mouse cables run across the table to the rear trough. CRT power exits its rear, passes through the trough, clips to the rear-right table leg at Z=0.13, 0.34, and 0.55 m, then enters a 170 × 130 × 6 mm flush floor service box centred (0.62,0.34,0.003) m. All visible components have a supported surface or structural mounting; no cable hangs unsupported.

Walls are smooth warm-white matte painted board; floor is sealed honed concrete with quiet aggregate variation and a 3 mm perimeter movement joint. Two 3000 K ceiling track spots, CRI ≥95 and R9 ≥90, use 48° feathered beams at (−1.25,−1.10,3.0) and (1.25,0.15,3.0) m, aimed at the table and circulation floor. Use 80–120 lux on controls, dimmed to avoid screen glare; verify with a meter. Broad indirect room light supplies the soft fill shown in the visualization. Fixture housings, stems, cable routes, table legs, and floor contacts are modeled.

## Installation procedure and files

Prepare protected power and data service; level the table; fasten cable clips and trough; place and secure the CRT; connect headphones, keyboard, and mouse; focus fixtures; calibrate display; then run a full game, audio, and recovery test. Align table and monitor within ±3 mm, level to ±1 mm/m. Confirm electrical safety, CRT ventilation, accessible approach, cable restraint, and monitor stability at venue. Keep 18–24°C and 40–55% RH. Pack the CRT in its protective carton; pad table edges and pack peripherals separately.
