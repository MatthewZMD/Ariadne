## Ambient recordings

The environmental recordings under `public/audio/ambience/` are Creative Commons Zero recordings sourced from Freesound. Their titles, creators, original URLs, local filenames, and uses are documented in [`public/audio/ambience/AUDIO-SOURCES.md`](public/audio/ambience/AUDIO-SOURCES.md).

Although CC0 does not require attribution, provenance is retained as part of the artwork’s production record.
